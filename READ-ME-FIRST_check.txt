DIVIDENDII CHECK APP: 23 November 2026 changes (v1.0)

This zip holds ONLY the files that changed. Do not delete anything in your repo.
Copy each file over the file with the same name and path in your dividendii-checkv1 repo.
New files (they do not exist in your repo yet):
  - app/api/pilot/daily-check/route.ts
  - lib/pilot/alerts.ts

STEPS
1. Unzip this folder. Copy everything in it into your repo, choosing "Replace" when asked.
2. In Vercel, project settings, Environment Variables: add CRON_SECRET (any long random text, 32+ characters). Without it the daily check will not run.
3. Commit and push. Vercel builds by itself.
4. No database change is needed (you do NOT re-run supabase/pilot.sql).
5. Open /check/admin/settings and fill "Copy me on every live registrar email". Registrars can SEE this address (it is a visible CC).
6. Test with a TEST Paystack key first (mode stays test): pay once, check the forms arrive in the test inbox with the CC.
7. Only then go live (see CHECK-SETUP.md section 4, "Before the first live customer").

WHAT CHANGED (short)
 - One fee story everywhere; total shown on step 2 and at pay; 7.5% VAT now also added to the 5% success fee.
 - No double payments: pressing Pay again checks the earlier payment first; a second paid payment is recorded and you are emailed.
 - Honest "Sent": a registrar address the mail server refuses now shows Not sent. A daily 07:30 email lists paid-but-not-sent forms.
 - Typed answers survive Back/Forward/reload/return from payment (this tab only; photo, signature and ticks are not kept).
 - Saves proof of consent (terms and privacy version, date, IP, device) with each submission.
 - Staff page: Sign out; refuses price 0; staff name on updates; updates cannot be removed; newest 1,000 of N; double-payment warning; Delete button (removes the record, its files, and if it was their last one, their account and feedback).
 - Power of Attorney wording; Vendii renamed Dividendii; BVN reason; plain-word help; input fixes; passwords of spaces or equal to the email are refused.
